## THE CORE STRATEGY

### The Bezos Principle: "Start with the Customer and Work Backwards"

You don't have a marketing problem. You have a DISTRIBUTION problem. The product exists. The farmers need it. The question is: **How do you get Wangari into the hands of 1,000 farmers who will actually use it?**

### The M-Pesa Lesson: Build Distribution Manually

Catherine Gichunge (original M-Pesa team) said:
> *"People say it is organic growth. It is not. We walked the streets. We set up one agent at a time."*

Your distribution strategy is the same:
1. **Walk the streets** (visit farms in person)
2. **Set up one farmer at a time** (onboard with patience)
3. **Build a network** (agents, cooperatives, agrovets)
4. **Let the network scale itself** (referrals, word of mouth)

## THE 4 DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS

### Channel 1: Field Agents (50% of users)

**The backbone of your growth.**

**Who to hire:**
- Agricultural college students (final year or recent graduates)
- Agrovet shop assistants who know farmers personally
- Retired extension officers with existing farmer relationships
- 4-K Club or FFA alumni

**How to find them:**
- Post on LinkedIn: "Wangari is hiring field agents for Kiambu/Nakuru. Commission-based. Help farmers manage their farms digitally."
- Ask at agrovets: "Do you know anyone who knows lots of farmers and is good with phones?"
- Contact agricultural colleges: Egerton University, JKUAT, University of Nairobi (Agriculture faculty)

**Compensation:**
| Action | Commission |
|---|---|
| Farmer signs up and uses app for 7 days | KES 500 |
| Farmer completes 30-day streak | KES 200 bonus |
| Farmer converts to paid plan | 10% of first 3 months' revenue |
| Cooperative onboarded (50+ members) | KES 5,000 flat |

**Agent daily routine:**
```
8:00 AM — Check Wangari Agent Dashboard (which farmers are inactive?)
9:00 AM — Visit Farm 1 (onboard or re-engage)
10:30 AM — Visit Farm 2
12:00 PM — Visit agrovet (drop flyers, recruit new farmers)
2:00 PM — Visit Farm 3
3:30 PM — Visit Farm 4
5:00 PM — Enter data for farmers who can't type (at their farm or via WhatsApp)
6:00 PM — Update Agent Dashboard, submit daily report
```

**Target per agent:** 5 farms/day × 20 days = 100 farms visited, 50 successfully onboarded per month.

**With 2 agents:** 100 new users/month = 200 users in 2 months.

**Agent toolkit (what you provide):**
- [ ] Wangari Agent Dashboard access
- [ ] Printed "Quick Reference Cards" (WhatsApp commands, one per farmer)
- [ ] Branded t-shirt or cap (visibility + trust)
- [ ] Phone with data bundle (KES 2,000/month per agent)
- [ ] Demo script (what to say at each farm)
- [ ] Sample reports to show farmers

### Channel 2: Cooperatives (30% of users)

**The force multiplier. One meeting = 100+ signups.**

**How to approach cooperatives:**

**Step 1: Identify target cooperatives**
- Kenya Poultry Farmers Association members
- Kenya Dairy Farmers Cooperative Society
- Local SACCOs with agricultural portfolios
- County cooperative unions

**Step 2: Get an introduction**
- Find a member of the cooperative who can introduce you
- Or attend their AGM as a visitor
- Or contact the cooperative manager directly via phone

**Step 3: Request 15 minutes at a members' meeting**
- Don't ask for 1 hour. Ask for 15 minutes.
- Bring your phone with Wangari open
- Bring the hero case study video (play it on a projector or TV)

**Step 4: The 15-Minute Pitch**

```
MINUTE 1-3: THE PAIN
"How many of you know exactly how much you earned last month?
How many know your cost per bird? How many suspect feed is
going missing but can't prove it?"

MINUTE 4-7: THE SOLUTION (LIVE DEMO)
[Pull out phone, open WhatsApp]
"This is Wangari. I'm going to send it a message right now."
[Send: "eggs 40, mortality 1, feed 3 bags"]
"See? In 3 seconds, it tells me today's profit."
[Show the response on screen]

MINUTE 8-10: THE PROOF
"Let me show you what happened to [Pilot Farmer Name] in Kiambu."
[Play 60-second case study video]

MINUTE 11-13: THE OFFER
"For cooperative members: First 30 days FREE.
After that, KES 300/month — less than a bag of feed.
And your cooperative gets a dashboard to see all members'
performance."

MINUTE 14-15: THE ASK
"Who wants to try it right now? I'll set up your account
before you leave this room."
[Have Agent Dashboard ready to create accounts on the spot]
```

**Step 5: Follow up**
- Visit the cooperative office 1 week later to check adoption
- Send WhatsApp group message with tips and encouragement
- Present monthly performance report to cooperative leadership

**Target:**
- Month 1: 1 cooperative × 50 active members
- Month 2: 3 cooperatives × 100 active members = 300 users

### Channel 4: Content & Digital (10% of users)

**The cheapest channel. Takes longest to build but compounds over time.**

**Facebook Strategy:**
- Join every Kenyan farming Facebook group (there are dozens with 50K-500K members)
- Don't spam. Provide VALUE.
- Post format: "I helped a farmer in Kiambu discover he was losing KES 15,000/month on feed waste. Here's how we found out. [Story + screenshot of Wangari report]"
- Comment on other farmers' posts with helpful advice
- Share the case study video

**YouTube Strategy:**
- Upload 3-5 minute tutorial videos in Swahili
- "Jinsi ya Kufuatilia Matunda ya Kuku kwa Wangari" (How to Track Poultry Production with Wangari)
- "Jinsi ya Kujua Umeingiza Pesanga Ngapi" (How to Know Your Real Profit)
- Farmer testimonial videos

**WhatsApp Group Strategy:**
- Create "Wangari Farmers Kenya" WhatsApp group
- Post daily tips, market prices, pest alerts
- Let farmers share their wins and ask questions
- DON'T sell. BE HELPFUL. Sales follow trust.

**SEO/Blog Strategy:**
- Write articles answering questions farmers Google:
  - "How to calculate cost per bird in Kenya"
  - "Poultry feed calculation formula Kenya"
  - "How much profit does a layer farm make in Kenya"
  - "Best vaccination schedule for layers Kenya"
- Include Wangari mentions naturally within the content
- Target long-tail keywords with low competition

## PRICING STRATEGY

### The Hormozi Framework: Value-Based Pricing

**Don't price based on cost. Price based on VALUE.**

If Wangari saves a farmer KES 15,000/month on feed waste, then KES 1,500/month is a 10x return. That's a no-brainer.

### Pricing Tiers

| Plan | Price | Target | What's Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | KES 500/month | Small farmers, trial users | 1 hub of choice + WhatsApp bot + Basic reports |
| **Pro** | KES 1,500/month | Mid-size farms | 3 hubs + AI assistant + Advanced reports + Push notifications |
| **Enterprise** | KES 3,000/month | Large farms, agro-vets, cooperatives | All 7 hubs + AI + Priority support + Multi-user + API access |
| **Cooperative** | KES 300/member/month | Cooperatives (50+ members) | Pro features for each member + Cooperative dashboard |
| **Founder's Special** | 50% off forever | First 1,000 signups | Whatever plan they choose, 50% discount locked for life |

### Free Trial Strategy

**30-day free trial with NO credit card required.**

Day 1: User signs up → picks 1 module → enters first data
Day 7: Bot sends: "You've tracked 7 days! Here's your first weekly report."
Day 14: Bot sends: "Your profit this week: KES 8,400. Want to unlock more features?"
Day 21: Bot sends: "Trial ends in 9 days. Upgrade now to keep your data and reports."
Day 28: Bot sends: "Last chance! Upgrade today and get 50% off forever as a Founding Farmer."
Day 30: Trial ends → Data is preserved (not deleted) → Read-only access until they upgrade

**Key principle: NEVER DELETE FARMER DATA.** If they leave and come back 6 months later, their data is there. This builds trust and reduces churn anxiety.

## RETENTION STRATEGY

### The "7-Day Hook"

**If a farmer uses Wangari for 7 consecutive days, they're 5x more likely to stay for 6 months.**

**How to achieve 7-day streaks:**

1. **WhatsApp daily reminder** (automated, 7am):
   > "Good morning, [Name]! Don't forget to log today's production. Send: eggs ___, mortality ___, feed ___"

2. **Streak counter** (shown in app and WhatsApp):
   > "🔥 7-day streak! You're on fire, [Name]!"

3. **Weekly reward** (gamification):
   > "🎉 This week you saved KES 2,100 vs last week! Keep it up!"

4. **Inactivity alert** (if no entry for 48 hours):
   > Agent gets notified: "Farmer [Name] hasn't logged data for 2 days. Please follow up."

5. **Monthly "Win Report"** (email or WhatsApp):
   > "📊 Your November Report:
   > Total eggs: 1,200
   > Total revenue: KES 72,000
   > Total costs: KES 58,000
   > Net profit: KES 14,000
   > vs. October: +18% profit"

## RISK REGISTER

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Farmers sign up but don't return after Day 3 | HIGH | CRITICAL | WhatsApp daily reminders, agent follow-ups, streak gamification |
| WhatsApp API costs spiral | MEDIUM | HIGH | Set message limits per user, optimize for efficiency, negotiate volume pricing |
| Agents don't perform | MEDIUM | HIGH | Daily reporting, commission structure tied to RETENTION not just signups |
| Competitors copy WhatsApp bot | LOW | MEDIUM | First-mover advantage, build data moat, focus on AI depth not just feature breadth |
| Internet outage kills offline users | MEDIUM | HIGH | Offline-first architecture, USSD fallback, WhatsApp (works on 2G) |
| Cooperative leadership changes, partnership dies | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Diversify across 5+ cooperatives, build direct farmer relationships too |
| M-Pesa API integration delayed | HIGH | MEDIUM | Manual reconciliation works fine for V1, auto-reconcile in Phase 2 |
