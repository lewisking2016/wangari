# Wangari — Revenue & Monetization Strategy

## Legal & Compliance Requirements

### Kenya Legal Framework

| Law | Relevance to Wangari | Action Required |
|-----|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Data Protection Act, 2019** | Collecting user/farm data | Register with ODPC, appoint DPO, conduct DPIA |
| **Consumer Protection Act, 2012** | Selling subscriptions | Clear pricing, 14-day refund rights, fair terms |
| **Electronic Transactions Act, 2008** | Online payments | Electronic contracts valid, M-Pesa compliance |
| **Computer Misuse & Cybercrimes Act, 2018** | Security obligations | Protect against unauthorized access, report breaches |
| **Kenya Information & Communications Act, 1998** | Telecom/Internet services | Licensing if offering telecom services |
| **VAT Act, 2013** | Charging VAT | Register for VAT if turnover > KES 5M/year |
| **Income Tax Act** | Revenue from subscriptions | File returns, withholding tax on digital services |
| **Companies Act, 2015** | Business entity | Register as limited company |

### International Compliance (for future expansion)

| Regulation | Applicability | Action |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **GDPR (EU)** | If serving EU customers | DPO, DPIA, consent management, data portability |
| **CCPA (California)** | If serving US customers | Do Not Sell, opt-out, privacy policy |
| **POPIA (South Africa)** | If serving SA customers | Similar to GDPR |
| **Nigeria NDPR** | If serving Nigerian customers | Data protection compliance |

### Registration Requirements

1. **ODPC Registration** — Mandatory under Kenya DPA 2019, Section 18
   - Register as Data Controller and Data Processor
   - Pay prescribed fees
   - Renew annually
   - URL: https://www.odpc.go.ke

2. **KRA PIN & Tax Registration**
   - Company PIN certificate
   - VAT registration (if turnover > KES 5M)
   - Withholding tax compliance

3. **Business Registration**
   - Register company with Registrar of Companies
   - Obtain relevant licenses from county government
   - Register with NHIF, NSSF for employees

6. **Affordable** — KES 999/month vs KES 5,000+ for competitors
7. **Mobile-responsive** — Works on any device
8. **Local data** — Data stored in Kenya/Germany (not US)
