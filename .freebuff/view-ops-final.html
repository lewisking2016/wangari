Dashboard — Farm Overview

Your landing screen after login. It is a live snapshot of the whole farm: today’s money in and out, money owed by customers, today’s egg production, mortality, low-stock warnings and pending orders.

How it works
  1. Read the overview cards at the top: money in today, money out today, total customer credit owed, overdue credit, eggs produced today, mortality today, low-stock items and pending orders.
  2. Check the charts for sales and production trends so you can see whether the business is growing.
  3. Review the recent orders list — open each one to verify payment and update its status (pending → paid → shipped → completed).
  4. Click any card or button to jump straight into the relevant module for detail.
Good to know
  • The dashboard is read-only — all changes happen inside the modules.
  • If you land here with a yellow notice saying you lack permission, that module was locked for your role. Ask the Super Admin to grant it under Settings → Roles & Permissions.

Quick Actions Menu

A shortcut menu in the top bar of every admin page. It lists that page’s main add/edit forms and exports, so you never have to hunt for the button.

How it works
  1. Look for the Quick Actions button at the top right of the page.
  2. Click it to open the menu — it shows shortcuts relevant to the page you are on (for example “Add Product” and “Export Products CSV” on the Products page).
  3. Click a shortcut — it opens that page’s own form/modal directly (e.g. Add Product opens the product form).
  4. Links that export (e.g. “Export … CSV”) download the file straight away.
Good to know
  • The menu is different on every module — it always matches the page you’re viewing.
  • It works on phones too: the menu appears in the top bar above the drawer.

Flocks

Every group of birds on the farm is a flock (batch). Flocks track how many birds you started with, how many are still alive, and whether the flock is active, sold or archived.

How it works
  1. Click Hatch New Flock to open the flock form.
  2. Enter the flock/batch name and code, choose the type (Broiler / Layer / Chicks), the house and the initial bird count.
  3. Save — the flock appears in the list with its current count.
  4. As birds die or are sold, record mortality on the flock so the live count stays accurate.
  5. When a flock is sold out, mark it Sold (or Archive old flocks) so it stops appearing in day-to-day lists.
Good to know
  • Accurate starting counts matter — every production and profit figure is built on them.
  • Batches & Houses and Daily Production all link back to flocks.

Daily Production

Log what each flock produced today: clean eggs, cracked eggs, meat weight in kg, and feed consumed.

How it works
  1. Click Log Daily Yield.
  2. Pick the batch/flock and the date (defaults to today).
  3. Enter clean eggs, cracked eggs, meat weight (kg) and feed consumed for that day.
  4. Save — the record is added to the daily production table and feeds the dashboard and analytics.
Good to know
  • Log every day for reliable charts and egg totals.
  • Cracked eggs should also be captured in Losses & Quality so they are not double counted as production.

Vaccinations

Plan and track vaccines and treatments per batch, so no flock misses its schedule.

How it works
  1. Click Schedule Vaccine.
  2. Choose the batch, the age/day of the birds, the vaccine or treatment type and the date.
  3. Save as Scheduled.
  4. On the due date mark the record Completed — or Missed if it didn’t happen so you can reschedule.
Good to know
  • The calendar module can show upcoming vaccine dates at a glance.
  • Keep notes on reactions or issues — history is useful at the end of a cycle.

Batches & Houses

Manage production batches and the houses they live in, and log each day’s record per batch (eggs by grade, mortality, feed).

How it works
  1. Use New Batch to open a batch, and Add House to register a house/building.
  2. Select a batch and click Log Today’s Record to enter that day’s eggs (Extra Large, B14, B15 etc.), mortality and feed.
  3. Records accumulate per batch and power the production and analytics screens.
Good to know
  • Assign each flock to a house so production is traceable by location.
  • Keep one batch per distinct group of birds.

Health & Vet

A veterinary diary: record deworming, vitamins, antibiotics, observation and other treatments, and see vaccination/mortality snapshots.

How it works
  1. Click New Health Record (via Quick Actions or the page header).
  2. Choose the record type — deworming, vitamins, antibiotic, observation or other.
  3. Select the batch, date, dosage/description and any notes, then save.
  4. Review the summary cards (vaccinations this month, mortality this week, treatments logged) to spot problems early.
Good to know
  • Use the notes field to record batch numbers of drugs for traceability.
  • Serious issues are also a good candidate for a system alert.

Broiler Workflow

Weight-tracking for broiler batches: periodic weigh-ins tell you average weight per day so you know when birds are ready for market.

How it works
  1. Click Record Weigh-In.
  2. Pick the batch, the day number (age), the sample size (how many birds you weighed) and the average weight in kg.
  3. Save — the average grows over time and shows whether the flock is on target.
Good to know
  • Weigh a representative sample (e.g. 10 birds) rather than the whole flock.
  • Compare weigh-ins to the breed standard to decide when to sell.

Hatchery (DOC)

Track hatching: how many eggs were set, how many were fertile, how many hatched, the hatch percentage and where the day-old chicks went.

How it works
  1. Click New Hatch Record.
  2. Enter eggs set, fertile eggs, hatched chicks, hatch %, destination (e.g. own brooding or sold) and any cost.
  3. Save — hatchery history is listed and exportable.
Good to know
  • Hatch % is a key hatchery health indicator — track it over time.
  • Day-old chicks that go into the farm should become a new flock.

Feeding Program

Two parts: weekly feed standards per bird type (grams per bird per day) and daily feed allocations logged per batch.

How it works
  1. Set up standards with “Add Week” — bird type, week number and grams per bird per day.
  2. Each day, click Record Feeding, choose the batch and enter the kg fed.
  3. The comparison between standard vs actual shows over- or under-feeding.
Good to know
  • Feed is usually the biggest cost — reconcile it against feed purchases in Stores & Stock.

Losses & Quality

Two trackers: the egg loss log (broken, cracked, stolen, eaten, expired…) and raw-material quality tests (moisture, aflatoxin, purity…).

How it works
  1. On the Egg Losses tab click Record Loss.
  2. Choose the date, batch (optional), the loss type and crucially where it happened — During collection, On route (transport), At storage/farm or Other.
  3. Enter the quantity (eggs) and an optional value, add a reason, and save.
  4. Switch to the Quality Tests tab and click New Test to log a material test with a pass/borderline/fail result.
Good to know
  • Recording the stage (collection vs transport) shows you exactly where eggs are being damaged so you can fix it.
  • Every loss log line appears in the export CSV for monthly reporting.

Products Catalog

Everything sold on the website and at the farm gate: live chicken, eggs, day-old chicks and feed. Each product has a price, stock level, type and visibility toggle.

How it works
  1. Click Add Product to create a product: name, type (Live Chicken / Eggs / Chicks / Feeds), price, stock, description and image.
  2. For feed bags, link a production recipe so producing a bag automatically deducts the ingredients from raw-materials stock.
  3. Toggle a product Active to show or hide it on the public shop instantly.
  4. Use the search box and type filter to find products, then Edit or Delete from the row actions.
Good to know
  • Keep stock levels honest — the website shows real availability.
  • Use Quick Actions → “Export Products CSV” to back up or edit in bulk, then re-import via Bulk Import/Export.

Stores & Stock

Raw materials (maize, soya, premix…) with live stock levels in kg, unit prices, suppliers and low-stock alerts.

How it works
  1. Review the stock table — quantity, unit, cost per kg, category and alert status.
  2. Use Record Movement / New Movement to add stock in (delivery) or remove stock out (usage/sale).
  3. Manage suppliers on the suppliers tab so purchase orders can reference them.
  4. Watch the alert list for materials below their minimum stock level and re-order via Procurement.
Good to know
  • Stock is measured in kg — legacy tons are converted automatically.
  • Feed production and purchase orders update this stock automatically.

Feed Production

Build feed recipes from raw ingredients, then “produce” a batch — the system deducts the ingredients from raw-material stock and adds finished feed bags.

How it works
  1. Open the Recipes tab and click New Recipe.
  2. Name the finished feed (e.g. Layers Mash) and add each ingredient with its quantity per batch.
  3. Click Produce on a recipe, enter the number of bags, and confirm — ingredient stock is deducted and finished feed stock increases.
  4. Check the History tab to see every production run.
Good to know
  • Link the recipe to the matching product in Products so online feed sales stay in sync.

Egg Grading

Grade collected eggs by size every day — Peewee, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, Jumbo, plus the farm’s B14/B15 grades and Cracked — and record crates and damaged eggs.

How it works
  1. Click New Grading.
  2. Pick the date, batch (optional) and the grade (size).
  3. Enter total eggs, crates, and any damaged count (subtracted from total), plus notes.
  4. Save — one row per day per grade, listed with an Export CSV button.
Good to know
  • Grading data feeds pricing decisions per size.
  • Damaged eggs should also be logged as losses so they aren’t lost from the totals.

Sales & Finance Hub

The money nerve-centre: customer orders, sales summary, incoming payments, outgoing expenses and reports, all in tabs.

How it works
  1. Customer Orders tab: search orders, filter by status, and use Status to move an order pending → paid → processing → shipped → completed (or cancelled).
  2. Sales Summary tab: shows completed/paid orders as your sales register.
  3. Incoming Payments tab: click Log Payment to record cash, M-Pesa, bank transfer or cheque collections with status.
  4. Outgoing Expenses tab: embedded expenses list for farm spend.
  5. Reports tab: embedded analytics reports.
Good to know
  • Online customer orders appear here automatically.
  • Use the Quick Actions menu to jump to Cashbook, LPO, Credit or Bulk Sales.

LPO & Invoicing

Quotations, Local Purchase Orders (LPO) and invoices — one place for the whole document lifecycle, from a first quote to a paid invoice.

How it works
  1. Click New Document and choose the type: Quotation, LPO or Invoice.
  2. Fill the customer (name, phone, email, address), issue date and due/valid-until date.
  3. Add line items — description, quantity, unit, unit price — and optionally a tax rate % and discount; the total updates live.
  4. Save as Draft, then use the status selector in the table to move it along: sent → accepted → invoiced → paid (or cancelled).
  5. Click the eye action to open the print-ready document and press Print to produce a paper copy.
  6. Use Export CSV for a spreadsheet of all documents.
Good to know
  • A common flow: send a Quotation → customer raises an LPO → convert it to an Invoice → mark Paid when money lands.
  • You can edit a document at any stage; totals are recomputed from the items.
  • Every document appears in the Activity Logs audit trail.

Costs & Profit

Log costs per batch (chicks, feed, drugs, labour, utilities, transport…) and see profit per batch and overall.

How it works
  1. Click Add Cost.
  2. Pick the batch, date, cost type, description, quantity/unit and total cost, plus how it was paid.
  3. Save — the batch cost summary and profit calculation update.
Good to know
  • Capture every cost, including small ones, or profit will look better than it is.

Cashbook (Money Book)

A simple money in / money out ledger with daily balances — the farm’s day-to-day cash record.

How it works
  1. Click Money In to record income (source, amount, method, date, notes).
  2. Click Money Out to record spending the same way.
  3. The table shows today’s in/out and the running balance.
  4. Use Export CSV to download the ledger.
Good to know
  • Use it daily and it reconciles with the Sales & Finance hub at month end.

Customer Credit

Sell on credit and track what customers owe: balances, due dates and overdue amounts.

How it works
  1. Click Record Credit Sale.
  2. Pick or create the customer, enter the amount, due date and description.
  3. When the customer pays, record a payment against the credit — the balance reduces automatically.
  4. Watch the overdue list so money owed doesn’t get forgotten.
Good to know
  • Overdue totals also show on the dashboard.

Procurement (Purchase Orders)

Order raw materials from suppliers, then receive them — receiving automatically adds the quantity to stores stock.

How it works
  1. Click New Order, choose the supplier, order date and expected delivery.
  2. Add the materials with quantity, unit and unit price — the PO total updates.
  3. Save the order (draft/sent/confirmed).
  4. When goods arrive, mark the order Received — stock is added automatically.
  5. Manage suppliers on the Suppliers tab (name, contact, phone, email, lead time).
Good to know
  • Receiving recalculates moving average costs automatically.

Daily Reconciliation

Each day, match what was produced and sold: crates sold, sales amount and notes — a daily close-out.

How it works
  1. Click Record Daily Sales.
  2. Enter the date, crates sold, sales total and any notes.
  3. Save — the summary cards (today, this week) update.
Good to know
  • Do this at close of day to keep daily totals trustworthy.

Bulk Sales & Walk-in

Quick sales for walk-in customers and bulk buyers, with a customer list so repeat buyers are one click away.

How it works
  1. Click New Sale — pick or add the customer, date, items/amount and payment method.
  2. Use Add Customer to register walk-in customers for future sales.
  3. The sales table and Export CSV give you the day’s bulk totals.
Good to know
  • Combine with Customer Credit when a buyer takes goods on credit.

Analytics & Charts

Visual dashboards: KPI cards and charts for sales, production, costs and performance.

How it works
  1. Read the KPI cards at the top for headline numbers.
  2. Use the charts to compare periods and spot trends.
  3. Click the refresh button to reload the latest data.
Good to know
  • Charts only reflect what has been logged — keep the modules fed with data.

Bulk Import / Export

Move data in and out as CSV: download backups and templates, or mass-create products, customers, raw materials, flocks and expenses.

How it works
  1. The stats row shows live record counts (products, orders, customers, flocks).
  2. Export — pick an entity card and click Export to download a clean CSV of that data.
  3. Import — choose the entity type, then drag a CSV onto the dropzone (or browse).
  4. The format panel shows the exact columns expected; use Download CSV template to get a headers-only file to fill in.
  5. Click Import — the records are inserted and you get a count.
Good to know
  • Importing a product/customer with an existing name/email updates it instead of duplicating.
  • Always download a template first so the columns match exactly.

Staff

Create and manage staff accounts (who logs into the admin panel) with their roles and contact details.

How it works
  1. Click Add Staff and fill username, email, names, phone, role and an initial password.
  2. Save — the staff member can now log in with their own account.
  3. Use Edit to change details, role or reset their password.
Good to know
  • Choose the role carefully — it decides which modules the person can open (see Roles & Permissions).

Customers & User Accounts

Every account in the system: edit your own admin profile, create customer/team accounts, change roles, reset passwords and remove accounts.

How it works
  1. My Admin Account — update your own name, email and password.
  2. Add User — create an account with a username, email, initial password and role (Customer, Sales Staff, Stock Manager, Farm Manager, Super Admin).
  3. Change a user’s role from the dropdown in the list.
  4. Use the key icon to reset a password, or the trash icon to delete an account (never your own).
Good to know
  • Roles decide module access — fine-tune them under Settings → Roles & Permissions.

Tasks

Assign farm tasks with a type, date and owner so nothing gets forgotten.

How it works
  1. Click Add Task.
  2. Enter the task, type, date and who it is assigned to.
  3. Mark tasks done as they are completed; the list shows open vs completed.
Good to know
  • Tasks appear on the Calendar by due date.

Messages

Internal messaging: compose and send messages between staff accounts, with an inbox.

How it works
  1. Click Compose, pick the recipient, subject and message, and send.
  2. Read replies in the inbox; messages show status (read/unread).
Good to know
  • Messages stay inside the system — no need for personal phones.

Calendar

A month/week/day view of farm tasks and health/vaccination schedules.

How it works
  1. Switch between Month / Week / Day views.
  2. Click an event to see its details and due date.
  3. Add events from Tasks and Vaccinations automatically; use the page to keep an eye on what’s coming.
Good to know
  • Use it at the start of each week to plan feeding, vaccination and sales activity.

Dropdowns

Edit the options inside system dropdowns (product categories, units, statuses, roles, chicken sizes…) without touching code.

How it works
  1. Pick a group from the list (e.g. Product Categories, Units, User Roles).
  2. Add a new option with Add Option (label + value + sort order).
  3. Edit, enable/disable, or delete custom options per row.
Good to know
  • Protected system options cannot be deleted — disable them instead to keep the backend stable.

App Settings

General farm configuration: farm name, contact email and phone used across the system.

How it works
  1. Edit the fields (farm name, contact email, phone number).
  2. Click Save Changes to apply them.
  3. The hub also links to Dropdowns, Activity Logs and Roles & Permissions.
Good to know
  • These details appear on printed documents and the public site, so keep them current.

Activity Logs

A complete audit trail of everything that happens in the system — who did what and when.

How it works
  1. Open the list — every major action (logins, product changes, orders, LPOs, imports…) is recorded with the user and timestamp.
  2. Use it to investigate changes, track who recorded what, and audit the team.
Good to know
  • Logs are append-only — they can’t be edited, which keeps them trustworthy.

Roles & Permissions

Control which modules each role can open: a matrix of roles × modules with separate “view” and “edit” checkboxes.

How it works
  1. Open the card for the role you want to change (Super Admin, Farm Manager, Stock Manager, Sales Staff, Customer).
  2. Click Show / hide modules to expand the matrix.
  3. Tick view to let the role open a module, and edit to let it make changes.
  4. Click Save for that role — the change applies immediately to every user with that role.
Good to know
  • Super Admin always has full access (its checkboxes are locked).
  • A denied module disappears from that role’s sidebar, and direct links redirect to the dashboard with a notice.
  • Create accounts and assign roles under Team & Messages → Customers & User Accounts.
