## 1. Getting Started

### What is Wangari?

Wangari is a complete farm management system built for African agriculture. It helps farmers, managers, and workers track Livestock, Crops, Finance, Inventory, Customers, Workers, and Weather.

### System Requirements

- Any smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer
- Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge browser
- Internet connection (works on 3G/4G/WiFi)
- No app download needed - everything runs in your browser

### Subscription Plans

| Plan | Price | What You Get |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Free Trial** | Free (30 days) | Full access to all features |
| **Pro** | KES 1,500/month | 3 modules, 5 team members, 5 cows / 100 birds |
| **Plus** | KES 4,500/month | All modules, 15 team members, 200 cows / 2,000 birds |
| **Custom** | KES 12,000+/month | Everything unlimited, white-label, API access |

## 2. Registration and Account Setup

1. Go to https://wangari.imeantech.com
2. Click "Get Started" or "Sign Up"
3. Fill in: Full Name, Email, Phone, Username, Password
4. Choose role: Farm Owner or Join as Worker
5. Click "Create Account"

You get a 30-day free trial immediately - no card required.

**Google Sign-In:** Click "Continue with Google", select your account, complete the form.

---

## 3. Logging In

1. Go to https://wangari.imeantech.com/Frontend/pages/login.php
2. Enter email/username and password
3. Click "Log In"

- First time? You go to the Onboarding Wizard
- Returning? You go to your Dashboard
- Forgot password? Click "Forgot Password?" and follow the email link

## 4. First-Time Setup (Onboarding)

4-step wizard: Welcome > Farm Details > First Animals > Done!

- Step 1: Introduction (can skip)
- Step 2: Farm name, location, type (Poultry/Livestock/Crops/Mixed)
- Step 3: Add your first animals (type, count, breed)
- Step 4: Setup complete, links to key modules

## 6. Farm Operations (10 sub-tabs)

### Animals
Track every individual animal. Add with Tag, Name, Species, Breed, Gender, DOB, Group, Status. Edit/Delete.

### Groups
Manage flocks/herds: Name, Species, Type, Breed, Head Count, Location, Housing.

### Housing
Track buildings: Name, Code, Species, Type, Capacity, Location.

### Health Records
Log sickness/treatments: Date, Species, Subject, Type, Medicine, Vet, Cost, Status.

### Vaccinations
Schedule vaccines with built-in guides for Cattle (Anthrax, FMD, Brucellosis), Poultry (Newcastle, Gumboro), Goats (PPR, CCPP).

### Production
Daily log: Eggs, Milk, Weight, Feed used, Mortality per group.

### Breeding
Track mating/pregnancy/births: Dam, Sire, Expected Birth, Status.

### Feeding
Track feed consumption with reference standards.

### Poultry Tools
Batch Management, FCR Calculator, HDP Calculator, Batch Profitability.

## 17. AI Assistant (Wangari AI)

Click "Ask Wangari AI" in sidebar.

**Can do:**
1. Farm questions: "How much did I sell?", "Who owes me credit?"
2. Calculations: "What is 30*1600?", "Calculate FCR"
3. Farming advice: "Vaccination schedule?", "How to treat mastitis?"

**Modes:** Plan (data + AI knowledge), Work (takes actions - Plus only)

**Limits:** Free/Pro = 40/day, Plus = Unlimited

**Features:** Thinking animation, response timer, context counter, suggestion chips

## 22. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Cannot log in | Check credentials. Click Forgot Password |
| Page is blank | Refresh (Ctrl+F5) |
| Data not saving | Check required fields and internet |
| AI not responding | Daily limit reached. Try tomorrow or upgrade |
| Payment failed | Check M-Pesa/card balance. Try different method |
| Cannot see module | Plan may not include it. Upgrade |
| Slow loading | Check internet. Close other tabs |
