# Wangari Demo Video — Talking Points Script

Use this while recording the demo video (`www.mp4`). Lead with offline-first — it's the feature no competitor can claim and the one every rural farmer feels daily.

---

## 🎬 Opening Hook (0:00–0:15)

> "You're at the shamba, a customer buys five trays of eggs... and you have no bundles. **With Wangari, that sale is never lost.** Watch this."

**On-screen action:** Airplane mode ON. Record a sale. Show the confirmation appear instantly.

---

## 📴 Scene 1 — Works Without Bundles (0:15–0:45)

**Talking points:**
- "Turn on airplane mode — Wangari still works."
- "Record the sale, record today's eggs, note an expense. **Everything saves on your phone instantly.**"
- "See the amber banner? 'Data saved on your device — 3 records waiting to sync.' Nothing is ever lost."
- "Other apps just show an error. Wangari keeps working."

**On-screen action:** Record 2–3 records offline (sale, output, expense). Point at the offline banner and the pending count.

---

## 🔄 Scene 2 — Auto-Sync Magic (0:45–1:10)

**Talking points:**
- "Now I get bundles again... and **watch — no button to press.**"
- "Green banner: '3 saved records synced successfully.' Done. By itself."
- "And here's the clever part: even if you record the same thing twice while offline, **Wangari never double-counts.** Your books stay perfect."

**On-screen action:** Airplane mode OFF. Capture the sync banner and success message. Open Sales to show the records are there.

---

## 📲 Scene 3 — Installs Like an App (1:10–1:35)

**Talking points:**
- "No Play Store. No 50-megabyte download eating your bundles."
- "Tap 'Add to Home Screen' — Wangari installs like a real app. Full screen, Wangari logo, opens instantly."
- "It uses less than 2 MB of storage. Works on any phone, even a Tecno."

**On-screen action:** Chrome menu → Add to Home Screen → open the installed app full-screen.

---

## 🐔 Scene 4 — The Full Farm (1:35–2:20)

**Talking points:**
- "And once you're online, Wangari runs your whole farm:"
- "Animals — every flock, vaccinations due, mortality."
- "Crops — planting to harvest, even which block makes you money."
- "Money in, money out — real profit, not guesswork."
- "Workers — attendance, wages, who logged what."
- "And the daily digest email every morning: what needs your attention today."

**On-screen action:** Quick tour: Dashboard → My Animals → My Crops → Income & Expenses → Workers.

---

## 💬 Closing (2:20–2:40)

> "Wangari Farm OS. Built for Kenyan farms — **works without bundles, syncs by itself, and shows you your real profit.** Free for 14 days. Link below."

**On-screen action:** Dashboard with the profit chart visible. End card: wangari.imeantech.com + "Start Free — No Card Needed".

## ⚠️ Recording tips

- Record the offline scene **first** (Scene 1) — it's the wow moment; if the video gets cut short, the differentiator is already shown.
- Use the demo farm with real-looking data (birds, sales history) — empty dashboards kill trust.
- Show the amber → green banner transition clearly; it's the visual proof of "syncs by itself."
- Keep total length under 2:45 — completion rate drops hard after 3 minutes.
