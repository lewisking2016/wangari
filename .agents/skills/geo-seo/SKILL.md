# Geo-SEO & Structured Data Guidelines

## 1. Localized Geo Meta Tags
- `geo.region`: `KE`
- `geo.placename`: `Nairobi, Kenya`
- `geo.position`: `-1.286389;36.817223`
- `ICBM`: `-1.286389, 36.817223`
- `theme-color`: `#166534`

## 2. JSON-LD Schema Types
- **SoftwareApplication**: Name, Category (`BusinessApplication`), Operating System, Price in KES.
- **FAQPage**: Rich snippet question & answer structured data.
- **Organization**: iMeanTech brand link, logo, contacts.

## 3. High-Intent Geo Keywords
- `farm management software Kenya`
- `poultry record keeping app Kenya`
- `farm profit calculator KES`
- `offline farm management app`
