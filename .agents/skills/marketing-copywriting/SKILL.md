# Marketing Copywriting Guidelines (SaaS & Farm Tech)

## 1. Value Proposition Hierarchy
- **Primary Outcome**: "Stop Guessing. Start Knowing." -> Know your net profit in KES in 30 seconds.
- **Objection Killers Upfront**: "Works 100% Offline", "No Credit Card", "Free 14-Day Trial", "M-Pesa Ready".

## 2. Copy Principles
- Use active, direct verbs ("Track", "Log", "Calculate", "Prevent Loss").
- Avoid generic tech jargon. Use concrete numbers ("< 3 Taps", "30 seconds", "KES 1,500/mo").
- Honest, transparent tone — no inflated claims or fake metrics.
